ACME CORP — REMOTE WORK & EXPENSE POLICY

1. Remote Work Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed 90 days of employment are eligible to
work remotely up to 3 days per week. Managers must approve remote work arrangements
in writing via the HR portal. Contractors and interns are not eligible for remote work
unless explicitly approved by the VP of Operations.

2. Equipment Stipend
Employees approved for remote work receive a one-time equipment stipend of $500,
administered by the Finance department. The stipend can be used for desks, chairs,
monitors, or ergonomic accessories. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.

3. Expense Reimbursement
Travel expenses for client visits are reimbursed at 100% when pre-approved by a
manager. Meals during business travel are capped at $75/day. The Finance department
processes reimbursements within 10 business days of submission.

4. Roles and Responsibilities
- The HR Department owns the remote work approval workflow.
- The Finance Department owns equipment stipends and expense reimbursement.
- Engineering Managers are responsible for approving remote schedules for their reports.
- The VP of Operations, Priya Nair, has final authority over exceptions to this policy.

5. Product Support Note
Employees working remotely who need help with the "Aurora Laptop Stand" (an
ergonomic accessory eligible for stipend reimbursement) should contact IT Support,
led by Marcus Chen, for setup assistance.
