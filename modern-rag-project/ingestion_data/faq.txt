ACME CORP — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Who approves remote work requests?
A: Your direct Engineering Manager approves the schedule, but HR owns the overall
workflow and final record-keeping.

Q: I bought a Meridian Ergonomic Chair, can I get reimbursed?
A: Yes. The Meridian Ergonomic Chair qualifies under the $500 equipment stipend
described in the Remote Work & Expense Policy, since it is an approved ergonomic
accessory.

Q: My Aurora Cooling Pad stopped working, who do I contact?
A: Since the Aurora Cooling Pad is manufactured by NovaTech Industries, defect
reports should go to Vendor Relations, not IT Support, per the Support Escalation
Rule.

Q: Can contractors work remotely?
A: Not by default. Contractors need explicit approval from the VP of Operations,
Priya Nair.
